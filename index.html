Review notes
    
    
    Supported content & conversion limits
    
      
    Word: Paragraphs, bold text, links, nested lists, tables and embedded images are read in document XML order. Floating images may move. Headers, footers, footnotes, equations, SmartArt, charts and attachments are not exported.
    
      
    Excel: Choose one worksheet. The converter reads formatted values, saved formula results, cell bold and rich text. Images, charts and conditional formatting are not exported. Formulas are never calculated.
    
      
    Formatting: Merged cells are expanded into individual cells. Custom numbering becomes a standard numbered list. Unsupported text formatting is removed while the text is kept.
    
      
    Images: Replace every IMAGE_001_URL placeholder with its CMS image URL before publishing. Only adjacent images are grouped. Export URLs are not loaded as previews.
    
      
    Limits: 25 MB per file, 150 MB unpacked and 100,000 cells per table. Large files may temporarily slow down the browser.
